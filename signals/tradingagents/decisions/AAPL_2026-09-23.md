A close-based stop at $321.41 caps the loss at about 4.6% only in an orderly market. A repeat gap from $337 would cost close to 10%. The position has to be sized for that case.
(2) The Aggressive analyst's volume argument doesn't hold up. The 86.6M-share session on 09-18 closed lower ($337.00 to $336.13), and the quiet 31.7M-share session on 09-23 is a single day near the low end of September's range.
(3) Momentum is cooling. The MACD histogram fell from 1.68 to 1.20, a 22% drop in the last session alone. The MACD line flattened around 6.2, and price was turned back near the $345 upper Bollinger band on 09-22.
(4) Valuation is unknown. The 40x trailing P/E cap only works if someone checks the multiple, and no price-based metrics are available for this date. The 29% EPS figure is one quarter's year-over-year growth, and Q4 FY25 is missing from the data, so we can't calculate trailing EPS growth.
Because of these points, I'm rejecting the trader's full Buy and the larger 0.5x starter.

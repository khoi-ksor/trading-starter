**Rating**: Overweight

**Executive Summary**: Overweight NVDA, built in stages. Don't buy at $224, which is near resistance. First add about 0.4x of a standard allocation if the stock holds 217–219 on a daily close, sized so a stop-out costs no more than your normal per-position risk. Exit on a daily close below 205. Keep total exposure at or below 1.0x until the earnings date and rate data are confirmed. Add a second, partial tranche only on a close above 230 with above-average volume and a MACD histogram turning positive. Add a third on a held test of the ~209 50 SMA. Holders already at or above full size should hold, and can trim 10–20% if 228–230 rejects the stock again on heavy volume. Horizon is 3–6 months.

**Investment Thesis**: The business case is strong and no analyst disputed it. Q2 FY27 revenue was $96.2B, up 106% YoY and faster than the prior quarter. Gross margin was 75%, operating margin 66%, and H1 free cash flow was about $70B with little capex. Even after stripping out non-operating gains, the research manager puts the stock at roughly 25–27x run-rate earnings, which is reasonable for triple-digit growth. All three moving averages are rising, in the right order, and the stock has made higher lows since 189.80. That supports a positive rating.

The conservative analyst made several good points. The Aug 27 rejection at 230.21 came on 299M shares, and the bounce came on lower volume. RSI and the MACD histogram have been weakening for weeks while price made new highs. The plan's 230 breakout add contradicted its own 'don't chase the band' logic. The earnings-date safeguard relied on a date nobody has confirmed. Cash conversion fell to about 63% of net income. Without valuation, rates or news data, conviction stops short of Buy.

The conservative remedy goes too far, though. A stop just under the 209 50 SMA would likely be hit by normal swings when daily ATR is 7.57. The 7% loss figure assumes the full 1.25x is bought at once, which the staged plan never does. The neutral analyst's version fixes both problems while keeping the plan pointed up. It uses a daily-close stop at 205, sizes positions from the risk budget, and requires the MACD to confirm before the 230 add. It also caps exposure at 1.0x until the gaps are filled. The neutral analyst also suggested that Aug 27 may have been the earnings reaction, since the 10-Q covers the quarter ended Jul 26. That would make the earnings cap less binding, but it is unconfirmed and must be checked before acting on it. Gap risk below 205 remains, so keep sizes modest.

What would change the call:
- Up to Buy: a held breakout above 230 with positive MACD, plus confirmation that earnings have passed and rates are not hostile.
- Down to Hold or Underweight: a daily close below 205, especially below the ~196 200 SMA, or two or more failures next quarter. Those checks are cash flow at 80% or more of net income, receivables and inventory not growing faster than revenue, gross margin of at least 74%, and continued quarter-on-quarter revenue growth. Bad export-control news would also count.

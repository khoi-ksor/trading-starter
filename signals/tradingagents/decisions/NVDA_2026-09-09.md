The position stays below full size, which is why this is Overweight and not Buy, for four reasons the Conservative and Neutral analysts made well. (1) Cash conversion: Q2 operating cash flow was ~$24B against $59.7B of net income (~40%), down from ~$50B in Q1, while liabilities rose ~43% in the quarter against ~17% equity growth. The Aggressive analyst says this is payables timing. That is plausible but unchecked. (2) Valuation: we have no multiple and no market cap after a ~21% run from 189.80 to 230.10 in five weeks, so we can't tell how much of this growth is already priced in. (3) Momentum is slowing: the MACD histogram halved from 0.84 to 0.42 over three sessions, and RSI fell from ~60 to 54 while price closed higher on 09-04, a mild bearish divergence. (4) The 230 area has turned buyers away twice: the 08-27 spike on ~299M shares reversed to 217.31 the next day, and on 09-04 price rejected a 234.50 intraday high.

I side mostly with the Neutral analyst. The Conservative plan of buying only at ~218 risks never filling while price holds above the 10 EMA, and missing the entry has a real cost in a stock growing this fast. The Neutral analyst's most useful point is that the cash-flow question can be checked in the existing filing now, not after a later 10-Q. So the reconciliation gates every add beyond the first tranche, not just the last one. Since macro data (rates, CPI, VIX) and news or sentiment data were unavailable, stay near the 50% end of the cap until they are checked.

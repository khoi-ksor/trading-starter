**Rating**: Hold

**Executive Summary**: Hold BTC-USD. If you already own it, keep your normal position size and don't add near 83,300. If you're flat, the most to buy is a small starter, about 25% of your normal size, and only after a daily close holds in the 80,280–80,900 zone. If you want a stop wider than about 79,000 (for example 76,400), shrink the position so the dollar loss stays the same, around 9–10% of normal size at 76,400. Go back to full size on a daily close above 84,500 with above-average volume, and up to about 1.25x on a close above 86,603 with follow-through. Cut to about 50% on a daily close below 80,280 (or below 80,892 with the Bollinger bands widening), and to 25% or out below 76,400. Re-check in 3–5 sessions.

**Investment Thesis**: The longer-term trend and the short-term momentum point in opposite directions, and neither has won yet. No buy or sell signal has been hit, so Hold is the call.

Bullish evidence:
- The 50-day average (80,280) and 200-day average (71,729) are both rising.
- The 50-day is above the 200-day, and price is above both.
- MACD is still positive overall.
- Swing lows have been rising since August.

Bearish evidence:
- The Oct 7 candle opened near 85,555 and closed near its low at 83,276, on 38.7B volume against 25.2B the day before. The conservative analyst is right that this is selling into the close, not a flush-out low.
- Price closed below the 10-day EMA (84,557), the Bollinger middle (84,180) and a falling volume-weighted average.
- MACD has crossed below its signal line (histogram -278).
- Every close since the late-September high has failed to clear 86,603.

Why not Buy or Overweight: buying here offers only about 1:1 reward-to-risk. The aggressive plan improves the ratio to about 1.5x by moving the entry to 80,500 and the stop to 76,400, but it also raises size to 50% of normal. That makes the dollar loss per normal-size unit about 2,050 instead of about 375, roughly 5.5 times larger. It would also put size into the 80,280 zone where our own rules say to cut. The conservative and neutral analysts were right to reject it.

Why not Underweight: the bear's own cut signal, a daily close below 80,280–80,900, hasn't happened. Price is still about 2.9–3.4% above it. Trimming now would be acting before our own signal.

What I took from each analyst:
- The aggressive analyst's best point: a 79,000 stop on an 80,500 entry is only about 1,500 away, less than one day's typical range (ATR is 2,164), so routine price swings could hit it. The neutral analyst called that 1.5–2 ATR; that's only true measured from today's 83,276 close, not from the entry zone.
- The fix, as the neutral analyst argued: keep the dollar loss the same and let position size absorb any wider stop. If you're flat, buy in tranches: a starter on a confirmed hold of the zone, then add on a reclaim of 84,500 and on a break above 86,603.
- The narrowing Bollinger bands only say a big move is coming soon, not which way.
- The Saylor headline is undated and unverified, so I gave it no weight.

Missing data: macro and FRED, ETF and on-chain flows, and two of three sentiment sources. That keeps conviction low and argues for total exposure at or below normal until those inputs are available.

What would change the call:

- Downgrade to Underweight on a daily close below 80,280 or 80,892 with the bands widening, and toward Sell below 76,400.
